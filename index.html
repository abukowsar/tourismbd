Suggested routes

Choose the Bangladesh that fits your trip

3-4 days

Dhaka Heritage Weekend

Old Dhaka food lanes, Lalbagh Fort, riverboat views, Sonargaon crafts, and a calm evening on the Buriganga.

5-6 days

Sylhet Tea and Wetlands

Tea estates, Ratargul swamp forest, Jaflong stone country, village lunches, and soft adventure around green hills.

6-8 days

Cox's Bazar to Saint Martin

Beach sunsets, seafood markets, Himchari viewpoints, marine island stays, and relaxed coastal travel.

7-9 days

Sundarbans River Expedition

Launch cabins, mangrove creeks, wildlife watchpoints, forest permits, and slow mornings on tidal water.

Places to feel the country

From capital rhythm to open water

Dhaka

Food, rickshaw color, museums, river life, and layered Mughal and colonial history.

Sylhet

Tea valleys, wetlands, shrines, hill-edge drives, and quiet rural hospitality.

Cox's Bazar

Long beaches, sunrise markets, marine drives, seafood, and easy resort breaks.

Sundarbans

Mangrove forest, boat cabins, birdlife, watchtowers, and rare silence near the Bay.
